# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for participants of
all backgrounds, languages, identities, experience levels, and institutions.

Be precise, constructive, and patient. Critique evidence and implementations,
not people. Respect confidentiality and do not pressure anyone to disclose
laboratory or customer data. Misrepresentation of test results, affiliations,
adoption, or project metrics is unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when behavior
threatens people, confidentiality, legal obligations, or project integrity.
Report conduct concerns through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
until a dedicated conduct address is published.
